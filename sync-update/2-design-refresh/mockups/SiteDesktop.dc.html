What is dropshipping?

Sell products. Never hold stock.

Dropshipping is a simple business model to understand: you list products, take orders, buy from a supplier and keep whatever margin is left after costs. No warehouse and no upfront bulk inventory. Depop gives you access to a large, established marketplace of buyers. Your Sync dashboard walks you through the process, step by step. Like any business, it takes work, and results vary from person to person.
