Tutorial kit

Five pieces, used the same way in every tutorial. One action per step: the glowing thing is the only thing to tap, and tapping it is how you move on.

Top up & earn XP
1 · Spotlight + hand The target lifts above an 80% dim, glows in gold and pings outward. The hand sits just off the target and taps every 1.1s. Never spotlight more than one thing.
You got a crate! Tap it. Every level gives you one.
2 · Instruction card + progress Pinned to the top or bottom of the screen, whichever side the target isn't on. An icon for the action, one bold line that says what to tap, one plain line that says why. The dots show how far through you are.
Nice! Next step unlocked
3 · The “Nice!” moment After every step: a gold check pops in with a chime for about 0.9s, then the next step starts by itself. Short praise words: Nice!, Found it!, Shipped!, +$50!
Welcome to Sync Rewards Let's go Skip for now
4 · Start and finish cards Start says what you'll learn and how long it takes. Finish recaps the loop in 3 icons. Skip and Replay are always offered.
Practice mode · no payment Practice crate Anything that would cost money or roll real odds runs as practice and says so. The tutorial's $50 crate is a demo; real crates use the published odds.
5 · Practice mode Members can do the real action without paying or being misled.
Rules for every step
WordsBold line: 8 words max, starts with the verb, names the button as it's written (“Tap Add to order”). Second line: 8 words max, the reason. One actionThe step moves on only when the real target is tapped. No Next buttons, except on the start and finish cards. Length5 parts, about 30 seconds. Rewards: XP → top up → pack → crate → collect. Portal: focus → step → products → checkout → support. SoundTap: a knock. Each “Nice!”: a short chime. Level-up and crate: the full crate sounds (crate-fx.js). Mute follows the member's setting. MotionWith reduced motion on: no pulsing or tapping, instant cards, crate goes straight to the reveal. WhenPortal: first login. Rewards: first time Rewards opens. Shown once; Replay lives in Support.
